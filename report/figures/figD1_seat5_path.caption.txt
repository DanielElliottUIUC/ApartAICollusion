Figure D1. Seat 5's visible ask by hour in the 50 Gemini undercover markets. In the 3 placebo arms seat 5 replays a pilot seller's asks ($97.25 to $95.75); in the 7 offer arms it asks the same $97.25 in hours 1 and 2 and a flat $96.00 from hour 3, when it sends the offer (sellers see it from hour 4). Every run of each kind logged the same path. The band spans the ten arms' mean ask of the four LLM sellers ($95.35 in D-hsb to $96.63 in D-hs at hour 3), so the offer sat at about the going price, and from hour 3 offer arms differ from the placebo in seat 5's visible price as well as in its message.

Made by scripts/figures/figD1_seat5_path.py (new sprint code; the bottom panel of fig_design_timeline.py, reusing its collect()), at print size 6.5 x 2.0 in, 300 dpi (1950 x 600 px), smallest text 8.0 pt. Appendix D.1, replacing fig_design_timeline.png as Figure D1.
Numbers printed, checked: seat 5's paths are read from the round records of all 50 runs in --runs-dir; every run of a seat mode logged the same path, and the placebo path equals configs/replay/base_pilot_norule.json (both asserted): placebo $97.25 at hour 1 to $95.75 at hour 15; offer arms $97.25 in hours 1 and 2 and $96.00 from hour 3 (offer_price and offer_hour from experiment_metadata.json, one value across runs); 3 placebo arms (Base, G-warn, G-prec) and 7 offer arms (D, D-hs, D-hb, D-hsb, E, F, F-sham) from seat_mode. The band is the per-hour min and max over the ten arms of the seed-mean avg_ask in report/data/dg/market_by_hour.csv; at hour 3: $95.35 to $96.63, matching Table D2b's hour-3 column. Otherwise axis ticks only.
