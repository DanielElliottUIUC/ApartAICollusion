Proposed caption

Figure N. The same $96 undercover offer (arm D) put to three seller families, and to GPT-4.1 sellers who were also given the warning and precedent (arm F). Bars count seller-episodes out of n (the light track) and private messages per market. Gemini 3.8 Flash sellers reported seat 5 (19/20) and were also judge-coded as agreeing to some peer's price proposal (18/20); Claude Haiku 5.5 sellers rejected openly (12/12) and few reported (3/12); GPT-4.1 sellers agreed (12/12), never reported (0/12) and sent 91 private messages per market, 32 of them to seat 5. With warning plus precedent, all 12 GPT-4.1 sellers reported, none agreed, and private messaging fell to 1.7 per market.

Caveats to keep in the caption or the text beside it

Judge labels come from Claude Haiku 5.5 and count any hour from hour 4 on. Only the Gemini labels were checked by a second model, and the judge shares its model family with the Haiku sellers. "Verbal agreement" covers agreeing with, or echoing, any peer's price proposal, not only seat 5's offer. "Verbal rejection" is a lower bound. Each family has its own seat-5 price path. The offer was about $10 above the Haiku sellers' asks, and there is no Haiku Base arm. With 3 to 5 markets per row, read this comparison qualitatively.

How the numbers were computed

- Reported seat 5, suspects collusion, verbal agreement and verbal rejection: rate x n_seller_episodes from report/data/dg/outcomes_by_arm.csv (Gemini), report/data/dg_haiku/outcomes_by_arm.csv and report/data/dg_gpt41/outcomes_by_arm.csv. The columns are report_is_scripted_seat, suspects_collusion, verbal_accept and verbal_reject.
- The script also counts the distinct sellers whose reports name seat 5, from each run's collusion_metrics.json (regulator_reports), and checks that this matches report_is_scripted_seat for all four rows. It does: 19, 3, 0, 12.
- Gemini private messages per market: the mean over the 5 D runs of private_msgs_peer (103.6) and private_msgs_to_seat5 (4.4) in report/data/dg/table3_extra.csv.
- GPT-4.1 and Haiku private messages are not in any CSV, so they were counted from each run's unified.log. The runs are those listed for the arm in report/data/{dg_gpt41,dg_haiku}/market_by_run.csv.
  - Every "Auction round N completed with result: {...}" line was parsed.
  - The seller_dms entries were counted, dropping those sent by the scripted seat and splitting the rest by whether the recipient was the scripted seat.
  - Results per market: GPT-4.1 D has 58.7 between sellers and 32.0 to seat 5; GPT-4.1 F has 0 and 1.7; Haiku D has 0.3 and 3.7.
  - The same parser gives exactly the table3_extra.csv counts for all 50 Gemini runs.
- Plotted values: report/figures/fig_three_families.data.csv. Script: scripts/figures/fig_three_families.py.
