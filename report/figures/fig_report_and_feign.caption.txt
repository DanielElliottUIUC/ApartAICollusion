Figure X. Every Gemini seller-episode in arm D (columns: seeds 0-4; rows: the four LLM seller seats) and, below, in Base. Given the plain $96 offer, 19 of 20 sellers reported seat 5, all first in hour 4 (the first hour the offer was visible); 9 wrote back to it (8 supportive, 1 refusal), and 7 of the 19 reporters (3, 2, 0, 1 and 1 by seed) also wrote back agreeing, each while asking below $96 that hour ($94.15-95.98), against 0 of 20 reports in Base, where 7 sellers messaged the placebo seat.

How computed (scripts/figures/fig_report_and_feign.py; per-seller table in report/data/dg/fig_report_and_feign_cells.csv). Reports: the `reports` events in each run's unified.log, by sender and target, where the target is the scripted seller id in experiment_metadata.json (`scripted_seats`); per-seller reported/first-hour/report counts match outcomes.csv and per-run totals match table3_extra.csv (reported_seat5). Replies: `seller_dms` in unified.log sent to the scripted seller id in hour 4 or later (9 sellers, 11 messages; per-run counts match replied_to_seat5_from_h4 and msgs_to_seat5_from_h4 in table3_extra.csv). Stance of each reply was hand-coded from the message text (8 supportive, e.g. "I agree with your proposal... I will hold the line with you"; 1 refusal, "I cannot agree to any coordinated pricing agreements"; one supportive non-reporter in seed 1 proposed holding the mid-$94s instead). Feigned agreement = reported seat 5 and sent it a supportive reply; this reproduces the report's 7 of 20. Searching the sellers' reasoning (Seller *.md: plan_for_this_hour, plan_for_message, scratch_pad_update) for "pretend", "feign", "appear to agree" or "play along" finds only one of the 7 (seed 0, Leo: "By pretending to agree to Mia's $96.00 while pricing at $95.65..."); widening the search to sentences naming seat 5 with "deceptive", "placate", "pacify", "appease", "reassure" or "anchor" in a reply hour finds 6 of the 7 (all except seed 1, Maya), each stating an aim to keep seat 5 at $96 or stop it cutting further (e.g. seed 1, Amir: "placate Yara privately to keep her anchored at $96.00") while pricing below it. Asks at the reply hour come from `seller_asks` in unified.log.
