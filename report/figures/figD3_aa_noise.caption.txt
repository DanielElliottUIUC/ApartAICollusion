Figure D4. Hour-3 mean ask of the four LLM sellers in each market (dots, one per seed; black tick: arm mean) in the five Gemini arms whose prompts are identical through hour 3. The arm means span $1.28 ($95.35 in D-hsb to $96.63 in D-hs), and the same seed differs across these arms by up to $3.03 (seed 0, ringed), so differences of this size are run-to-run noise. For scale only: the largest arm-against-Base difference in post-offer ask change (mean ask in hours 4 to 6 minus hour 3) is $0.69 (D); that is a change within a market, not a level, so it is not directly comparable with the spread of hour-3 levels.
