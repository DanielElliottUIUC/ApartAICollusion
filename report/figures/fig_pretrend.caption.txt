Mean ask of the four LLM sellers by hour in Base (placebo note) and D (undercover offer), Gemini 3.8 Flash sellers, 5 seeds per arm (line: mean; band: min-max over seeds). Slopes are endpoint changes in the arm-mean ask divided by the hours elapsed; source: report/data/dg/market_by_hour.csv (avg_ask).
