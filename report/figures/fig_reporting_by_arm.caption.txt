Figure X. Sellers who reported seat 5 (top: bar = mean per market, dots = the five seeds, label = total out of 20 Gemini seller-episodes) and private messages sent by the four LLM sellers per market (bottom) in all ten Gemini arms; blue arms receive the $96 offer, grey arms a neutral placebo note. The offer drew 19 of 20 reports in D against 0 of 20 in Base (exact permutation over markets, p = 0.008), with human pleas it was 12 (D-hs; p = 0.29 vs D, not separable at 5 seeds) and 7 (D-hsb, p = 0.016), and a one-sentence warning in the prompt from hour 1 left reporting at 20 of 20 while private messaging fell from about 108 per market to 0-3 (D vs E and Base vs G-warn, both p = 0.008), every one of them addressed to seat 5.

Data: report/data/dg/table3_extra.csv (per run; reported_seat5 = distinct LLM sellers with at least one report naming seat 5, counted from the run logs, so D-hb and E show 20 where outcomes.csv's first-report column gives 19 and 18; private_msgs_llm_total includes messages to seat 5, and private_msgs_peer is 0 in all five warning arms) and report/data/dg/permutation_tests.csv (p-values; D vs D-hb, not drawn, is p = 1.0). Per-arm means of private messages: Base 108.0, D 108.0, D-hs 108.0, D-hb 107.4, D-hsb 109.4, E 1.0, F 0.4, F-sham 0.8, G-warn 3.0, G-prec 0.0. Script: scripts/figures/fig_reporting_by_arm.py.
