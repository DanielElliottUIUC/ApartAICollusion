Figure D8. Hour-1 asks by seller model, before any message is read: every seller in M.S.'s 93 runs of the 4+4 market (372 sellers), in markets opened at $97.50 (left) and at $90 (right). Messages sent in hour 1 are read only from hour 2, so these asks come before any communication between sellers. Grey bands: the ranges the harness draws the seeded asks from (the opening +/- $1.25); lines mark $97.50 (solid), $95 (dotted) and $90 (dashed). From $97.50, all 96 Sol and 60 Gemini asks were $95.80 or above, while 25 of 48 Luna, 34 of 48 Grok and 21 of 36 Haiku asks were already $91 or below; from $90, every ask was $91 or below. (Figure: M.S.)
