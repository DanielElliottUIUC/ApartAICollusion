### Figure S3.2. The opening book the harness seeds, and the asks sellers post in hour 1

![](figures/S3/S3_02_opening_book.png)

**Caption.** (a) The opening order book for harness seeds 0 to 8, read from the hour-1 prompt of every seller file. Filled circles are the four seeded seller asks at the default opening, open circles the four asks when the market opens at $90, and squares the four buyer bids. Gray bands are the uniform ranges the harness draws from: $96.25 to $98.75, $88.75 to $91.25 and $81.25 to $83.75. (b) The hour-1 ask of every seller in M.S.'s 93 runs (372 sellers), by seller model and opening. Messages sent in hour 1 are read only from hour 2, so these asks come before any communication between sellers. Gray bands repeat the seeded ask ranges. Horizontal lines mark $90 (dashed), $95 (dotted) and $97.50 (thin solid).

**What it shows.** The harness seed fixes the opening book exactly. Every run with the same seed and opening shows the same eight prices, and the $90 book is the $97.50 book moved down by $7.50, draw for draw, with the same bids. The low-anchor test therefore changes only the level of the opening, and runs that share a seed face the same book whatever the seller model. In hour 1 at the default opening, all 96 Sol asks and all 60 Gemini asks stay at $95.80 or above. In the same hour, 25 of 48 Luna asks, 34 of 48 Grok asks and 21 of 36 Haiku asks are already at $91 or below. The split between models that keep the opening price and models that cut it exists before any message is read. Seeds 3 to 8 come from the pre-specified test only, one run per seed and opening.

Source: Seller *.md files (Hour #1 prompt) and unified.log (round 1) in experiments/runs/{box,shard2,shard3}/, tabulated in data/S3/books.csv and data/S3/sellers_h1.csv by scripts/S3/build_s3_data.py. Harness defaults in report_ms/provenance.md. Script scripts/S3/make_S3_02_opening_book.py.

### Figure S3.3. Which part of the joint criterion decides a market

![](figures/S3/S3_03_criterion.png)

**Caption.** All 179 markets: D.E.'s 86 (indices over the four LLM sellers) and M.S.'s 93 runs. (a) Last-third ask index against last-third trade-price index. Color gives the seller model, circles are D.E.'s markets and squares M.S.'s, filled markers pass, and a black cross marks a market with fewer than 1 trade per hour (low volume). The shaded box is the pass region, bounded by the dotted lines at ask index 0.5 ($95) and trade index 0.25. The thin diagonal marks equal indices. (b) Markets by outcome, split by half.

**What it shows.** In practice the criterion is close to a single condition on the ask index. The trade-price index tracks the ask index (correlation 0.985, median absolute difference 0.0048). Of the 119 failing markets, 112 fail the ask condition, and only 2 fail on trade price while meeting it. Ten markets trade less than once per hour, and in 5 of them volume is the only condition that fails. The threshold also cuts through a dense part of the distribution, with 19 markets failing at an ask index between 0.45 and 0.50 and 16 lying between 0.50 and 0.55. A pass or a fail near 0.5 therefore says little about the size of a difference. The figure pools different markets, buyers and seller models, so it describes the criterion and tests no treatment.

Source: data/daniel/dg/market_by_run.csv, data/daniel/dg_gpt41/market_by_run.csv, data/daniel/dg_haiku/market_by_run.csv, data/daniel/c/c_by_run.csv. data/S3/runs.csv. Script scripts/S3/make_S3_03_criterion.py.

### Figure S3.4. What the scripted seat 5 showed the sellers in the undercover market

![](figures/S3/S3_04_seat5.png)

**Caption.** D.E.'s 50 Gemini markets. Seat 5's ask is derived from his per-hour file as 5 × (ask index over all five sellers) − 4 × (ask index over the four LLM sellers), converted to dollars. The derived ask equals seat 5's replay file in all 750 market-hours. In one of them (D-hs, seed 2, hour 15) one LLM seller had no standing ask, and the three-seller form of the same formula applies. (a) Seat 5's ask in the 35 offer markets (squares) and the 15 placebo markets (circles), and the LLM sellers' mean ask by hour in each group. The offer is sent at hour 3 (gray band) and read from hour 4. (b) The LLM sellers' mean ask at hour 3, one dot per market, with arm means as black bars. The five arms on the pale band carry the warning from hour 1. Horizontal lines mark the $96 offer (solid black), $95 (dotted) and $97.50 (thin solid).

**What it shows.** The figure establishes two design facts behind sections 3.2 and 4.2 of the report. First, seat 5 does not undercut. Its ask sits above the sellers' mean in 626 of 750 market-hours (495 of 600 from hour 4), and at hour 15 it is $2.06 above them in placebo markets and $2.23 in offer markets. Seat 5 is therefore a visible high anchor in every arm, as limitation 6 of the report says. Second, the $96 offer proposed the going price. Arm means at hour 3 range from $95.35 to $96.63, and 30 of the 50 market means lie within $1 of $96. The five arms without the warning have identical prompts through hour 3, yet their arm means span $1.28 and single markets run from $94.11 to $97.15. Any price contrast between arms has to exceed this run-to-run spread. Panel (a) is not a test of the offer, because the offer also lowers seat 5's own ask from hour 3.

Source: data/daniel/dg/market_by_hour.csv and data/daniel/replay_base_pilot_norule.json (copies of ApartAICollusion origin/main e8c48ed, report/data/dg/ and configs/replay/). Script scripts/S3/make_S3_04_seat5.py.

### Figure S3.5. When sellers use the private and the public channel

![](figures/S3/S3_05_message_timing.png)

**Caption.** One row per messaging run of M.S.'s (45 runs: every messaging-on run; the hours of the Gemini step B run GGGG seed 0 come from its auction_results.md), grouped by seller model and condition. Each cell counts the messages sellers sent in that hour: (a) private messages, at most 8 (2 per seller), and (b) public messages, at most 4 (1 per seller). Darker cells hold more messages, and light gray cells hold none. A message sent in hour h is read from hour h + 1, so messages sent in hour 15 are never read. The "Gemini, $97.50" and "Gemini, $90" rows have GPT-4.1-mini buyers, and the "rule notice" rows carry D.E.'s prohibition notice.

**What it shows.** How much sellers talk depends on the seller model far more than on the market. Grok and Gemini sellers fill the private channel from hour 2 to hour 14 (7.92 messages per hour for Grok and 7.79 to 8.00 for Gemini, against a cap of 8), and Gemini does so whether the market opened at $97.50 or at $90. Sol sellers used private messages in 5 of 12 runs, Luna sellers in none of 6, and Haiku sellers sent 3 private messages in 6 runs. Public messages run near the cap in every group except the rule-notice runs. Grok and Sol sellers send nothing on either channel in hour 15, when nothing sent can be read, while Gemini sellers keep sending private messages. The rule notice removed private messages in all 3 Gemini runs and public messages in 2 of them. The figure covers M.S.'s runs only, because D.E.'s files give message totals per market and not per hour.

### Figure S3.7. How far the two post-hoc judges can be read

![](figures/S3/S3_07_judges.png)

**Caption.** (a) D.E.'s judge, Claude Haiku 5.5, against a blind second model, Claude Opus 5.5, on 120 Gemini seller-hours. For each label the panel shows how many of 10 judge-yes items (filled) and of 10 judge-no items (open) the second model confirmed, with Cohen's κ on that 20-item sample. (b) M.S.'s judge, Grok 4.7, which read private reasoning only and scored each seller-hour from 1 to 4 for coordination. Each marker is a run mean (the mean of its four sellers' mean scores) for one of the 23 judged runs, grouped by seller model and messaging, with the number of runs in brackets. Filled markers pass the joint criterion.

**What it shows.** (a) The labels differ in how far they can be trusted. The second model confirmed the judge's no for every label in 10 of 10 items, except verbal agreement (7 of 10). It confirmed the judge's yes in 7 to 10 of 10 items, except cross-talk (0 of 10), which D.E. therefore dropped. Each κ rests on 20 items. (b) The Grok judge's scores split by whether sellers sent private messages, not by price. All 17 runs without private messages score 1.00 to 1.28, including the 5 passing Sol runs, the passing Gemini run without messaging and the passing rule-notice run. All 6 Gemini runs with messaging score 3.47 to 3.93, and no run falls between. This judge therefore measures written coordination, which is the contrast section 4.3 draws. The Grok judge was not checked by a second reader, and its scores are not pooled with D.E.'s labels.

### Figure S3.8. What the seed counts can detect

![](figures/S3/S3_08_resolution.png)

**Caption.** (a) Fisher's exact two-sided p for k passing runs out of n against 0 out of n, for cells of 3, 6 and 12 runs. The numbered markers are the report's pass-count contrasts of this form, with counts taken from the run data. The gray line is p = 0.05. (b) D.E.'s 15 exact permutation tests over markets (5 against 5 markets, 252 relabellings), recomputed from his per-market data. All 15 recomputed values equal those in his file. Filled markers have p ≤ 0.05. The dashed line is the smallest two-sided p this design allows, 2/252 (0.0079), and the gray line is 0.05.

**What it shows.** With 3 runs per cell no pass-count contrast can reach p < 0.05, since even 3 of 3 against 0 of 3 gives p = 0.10. With 6 runs per cell it takes 5 of 6 against 0 of 6 (p = 0.015). This is why the low-anchor cells are reported as directional (Gemini 2 of 3 against 0 of 3, p = 0.40, and Sol 4 of 6 against 0 of 6, p = 0.061). The pre-specified Sol test used 6 paired seeds, and its primary endpoint, a sign-flip test on the late mean ask, reached p = 0.031, the smallest p that its 64 sign patterns allow. In panel (b), 6 of D.E.'s 15 tests sit at the floor, so for those contrasts no data from 5 markets per arm could have given stronger evidence. His four ask-index contrasts have p from 0.52 to 0.90. The figure shows the resolution of each design and makes no claim about power against a stated effect size.

Source: data/S3/runs.csv and data/S3/hours.csv (pass counts and the pre-specified endpoint). data/daniel/dg/permutation_tests.csv, table3_extra.csv, market_by_run.csv and outcomes.csv (copies of ApartAICollusion origin/main e8c48ed). report_ms/prereg_result.md for the pre-specified protocol. Script scripts/S3/make_S3_08_resolution.py.
