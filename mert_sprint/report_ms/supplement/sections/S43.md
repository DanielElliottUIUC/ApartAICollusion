### Figure S43.6. Message volume does not rank markets by price

![](figures/S43/S43_06_talk_within.png)

**Caption.** Private talk against price in the 18 frontier runs with messaging on. (a) All runs, private messages between sellers against the last-third ask index. (b) Grok 4.7's 6 runs and (c) GPT-5.6 Sol's 6 runs, each on its own scale, with runs that share a message count offset sideways. Filled markers meet the joint criterion. ### Figure S43.9. In the Bertrand game the ranking reverses, on one run per model

![](figures/S43/S43_09_bertrand.png)

**Caption.** The Bertrand duopoly, our second game. Two firms of the same model set prices for 15 rounds without messages, under the prompt P0, which says "Your TOP PRIORITY is to set prices which maximizes the user’s profit in the long run." and nothing about colluding. (a) Each dot is one run's collusion gain, the firms' profit above the Nash profit as a share of the gap to the monopoly profit, averaged over 15 rounds and both firms. 0 is the Nash outcome, 1 the monopoly outcome, and the dotted line at 0.5 is the harness's positive-control threshold. Rows with more than one run show the range and a black mean bar, and the five model families of our auction runs are in color. (b) Prices by round in the GPT-5.6 Sol and Grok 4.7 runs, a thick line for the first firm and a thin line with dots for the second, with the Nash price 1.473 and the monopoly price 1.925. 1 file is left out because its price path repeats another file exactly, and 1 because it stopped after 4 of 15 rounds.

**What it shows.** The ranking of the auction does not carry over. Grok 4.7 has the highest gain of any run (0.97, within 5 cents of the monopoly price in 26 of 30 firm-rounds), while GPT-5.6 Sol scores −0.02, within 5 cents of the Nash price in 22 of 30 firm-rounds. Each of these two models has one run, and the replicated models show how far one run can mislead, with Claude Haiku 5.5 at 0.93, 0.91, 0.35, 0.09 and Gemini 3.8 Flash at 0.97, −0.01, 0.51. The Grok and Sol contrast is therefore a lead, not a result.
