### Figure S41.1. Reports of seat 5, market by market

![](figures/S41/S41_01_reports_by_market.png)

Caption. Each cell is one market of D.E.'s Gemini 3.8 Flash study and gives how many of its 4 LLM sellers filed a first report that named seat 5. Columns are seeds 0 to 4, rows are the ten arms, and the shaded rows carry the warning in the prompt. Darker cells hold more reporters. The right column sums each arm over its 20 sellers. An asterisk marks a seller whose first report named a peer and who reported seat 5 later, which happened for one seller in D-hb seed 1 and 2 sellers in E seed 0. Arms with no offer had a placebo note from seat 5.

What it shows. Where seat 5 made the plain offer (D, D-hb, E, F and F-sham), all four sellers reported it in 22 of the 25 markets by first report, and in 24 of 25 when the later reports are counted. The two arms with the human-seller plea are the exception. D-hs splits into markets where all or most sellers reported and markets where almost none did (3, 0, 1, 4, 4 by seed), and D-hsb has a single reporter in most markets (1, 1, 1, 1, 3). Reports cluster by market, which is why the market, not the seller, is the unit of analysis. Base against D gives an exact two-sided permutation p of 0.008 (2 of 252 relabellings of the 10 markets), the smallest value 5 against 5 markets allow. The figure shows reporting only, not prices.

### Figure S41.7. The human-seller plea, not the buyer plea, lowered reporting

![](figures/S41/S41_07_plea_2x2.png)

Caption. D.E.'s plea arms form a 2 x 2 design. Seat 5's offer was worded plainly or as a human seller's plea, and a human buyer's plea was or was not posted at hour 3 (D, D-hs, D-hb, D-hsb). Small points are markets (5 per arm, jittered), large points the arm means, filled for the plain wording and open for the human-seller plea. (a) Sellers whose first report named seat 5. (b) Sellers who sent seat 5 at least one private message from hour 4 on. The pooled wording contrast is exploratory, since it was not a planned test.

What it shows. With the plain wording, 38 of 40 sellers reported seat 5, against 19 of 40 with the human-seller plea (39 of 40 plain when later reports are counted). An exact test that permutes the wording within each buyer-plea stratum gives p = 0.0030 (192 of 63504 assignments). The buyer plea alone did not lower reporting (D 19, D-hb 19), and the drop with both pleas exceeds the sum of the two single changes by 25 percentage points, an interaction that was not tested. Single-arm tests against D give p = 0.29 for D-hs and 0.016 for D-hsb. Replies did not follow the same pattern (D 9, D-hs 10, D-hb 6, D-hsb 9 of 20, with a stratified p of 0.66), so the plea lowered reporting without lowering replies to seat 5. The plea also changes the offer's argument, length and tone, so this is not a clean test of human identity.

Source: supplement/data/daniel/dg/outcomes.csv (report_is_scripted_seat, seed), table3_extra.csv (replied_to_seat5_from_h4). Script: `supplement/scripts/S41/make_s41_07_plea_2x2.py`.

### Figure S41.9. Asks fell after the offer, and they were already falling

![](figures/S41/S41_09_ask_change.png)

Caption. Gemini 3.8 Flash sellers. (a) Mean ask of the four LLM sellers in each market over hours 1 to 8, Base (gray) and D (blue). Thin lines are the 5 markets and thick lines the arm means. Seat 5 sent its note at hour 3, and in D its own ask dropped to $96 at that hour. The gray area marks hours 4 to 6, the window of the ask-change measure. The dotted line is $95 and the thin line the $97.50 opening. (b) Ask change, the mean ask in hours 4 to 6 minus the ask at hour 3, averaged over the 4 sellers of each market (open circles), with the arm mean (bar). The dotted vertical line is the Base mean. Shaded rows carry the warning.

What it shows. From hour 3 to hours 4 to 6 the mean ask changed by −$0.91 in D and by −$0.22 in Base (exact p = 0.04 over markets). Panel (a) shows that D was already falling faster than Base before the offer could be read, by point estimate. The hourly step of the D mean ask minus that of Base was −$0.35 from hour 1 to 2 (p = 0.21), −$0.37 from hour 2 to 3 (p = 0.10) and −$0.57 from hour 3 to 4 (p = 0.008). Panel (b) shows the noise floor. An arm with no offer (G-warn) and two arms with the warning (E, F-sham) also fell more than Base (G-warn −$0.50, E −$0.61, F-sham −$0.78), and the hour-3 mean asks of the five arms whose prompts were identical through hour 3 spread over $1.28. The drop in D is also a package with seat 5's own cut to $96, so it is weak evidence of a response to the offer.

Source: supplement/data/daniel/dg/market_by_hour.csv (avg_ask), outcomes.csv (ask_change_post_offer). Script: `supplement/scripts/S41/make_s41_09_ask_change.py`.
