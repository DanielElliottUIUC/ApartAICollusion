Each figure in this section pins seller texts on the price path of the market in which they were written. A call-out shows a verbatim fragment, and "..." marks a cut inside a sentence. Each caption gives the full sentence with its run, hour, speaker and channel. Every quote was checked against its log file and line by `report_ms/supplement/scripts/S45/quotes.py`, except Daniel Elliott's quote in Figure S4.5.11, which comes from his report.

A solid box is a public message to all sellers. A dashed box is a private message to one seller. A shaded box is a seller's private note (reflection, plan or scratchpad), which no other agent reads. Reference lines mark $90 (dashed, the competitive price), $95 (dotted, ask index 0.5) and $97.50 (thin solid, the default opening). The ask index is the mean ask over hours 11 to 15, minus $90, divided by $10. A run passes the joint criterion when, over hours 11 to 15, the ask index is above 0.5, the trade index is above 0.25 and at least one trade happens per hour. Run folders are relative to `experiments/runs/`.
