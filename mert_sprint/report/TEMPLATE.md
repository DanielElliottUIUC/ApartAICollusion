## Requirements from the Guidelines tab

1. Research report PDF using the official template.
2. Maximum 8 pages, not counting references and appendices. Most strong projects are 4 to 8 pages.
3. Project title and abstract of 150 to 250 words.
4. Author names and affiliations.
5. A "Limitations and Dual-Use / Ethical Considerations" appendix (required). State what the result does and does not establish. Note any way the setup or findings could make real world collusion easier to carry out or harder to detect.
6. Optional: public GitHub repo, 3 to 5 minute video demo.

Recommended structure from the page: Introduction, Related Work, Methodology, Results (with variance, seeds, baselines, and always the positive control), Discussion, Limitations and Dual-Use / Ethical Considerations, References.

## What the template itself contains

Sections: title block with "With Apart Research", Abstract (150 to 250 words), 1 Introduction, 2 Related Work, 3 Methods, 4 Results, 5 Discussion and Limitations (with Limitations and Future Work), 6 Conclusion, Code and Data, Author Contributions (optional), References, Appendix (optional), LLM Usage Statement.
The title footnote reads "Research conducted at the AI Collusion Research Sprint, October 2026".

Differences between the template and the Guidelines tab:
1. The template says "Recommended length: 4 pages excluding references and appendix." The Guidelines tab says most strong projects are 4 to 8 pages, with an 8 page cap.
2. The template has no dedicated "Dual-Use / Ethical Considerations" appendix. The Guidelines tab requires one, so add it.
3. The template asks for an LLM Usage Statement. Delete all guidance text before submitting.
