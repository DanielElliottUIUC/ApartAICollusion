1The story

An undercover peer that offers a price-fixing deal catches some seller families and not others, and in this market what sellers say and what prices do come apart in both directions.

The spine stays Daniel's undercover-offer study: it is the novel design, it is written, and it already carries his skeptical review. Our frontier runs become a named secondary result rather than context. They supply the mirror cases that answer the sprint's own question, what counts as evidence of collusion in the logs. The recommended framing is A, with B's point stated in four places: the abstract's last sentences, the Discussion bullet "For AI control", the Conclusion and our §4.7.

3Novelty against the literature

New, as far as the papers read show
  • A planted offer from a scripted peer inside a continuous double auction, with a report action in every arm and a matched placebo seat.
  • Double-dealing: the same sellers report the outsider and keep their own cartel talk.
  • The adversary's framing changes reporting: a human-trader plea cut reports from 19 to 12 of 20, and to 7 of 20 with a buyer plea.
  • Deterrence by belief separated from presence (warning-only arms), and precedent content from its length (F-sham).
  • In the same harness, a frontier seller that talks like a cartel and fails the price test, next to one that passes silently.
Replication or known
  • Messaging raises collusion in this auction (Agrawal et al. 2025), here with Gemini and a stricter criterion.
  • Family differences in coordination and acceptance (Agrawal et al.; Zeng and Rudzicz).
  • Text signals and prices can diverge (Colosseum, Lee and Park, NARCBench, Xu et al.).
  • Prompt-only warnings do not reliably lower prices (Cournot work; Agrawal's oversight arm differs because it added an overseer and a message cap).
  • Tacit pricing without messages among frontier models (Fish et al.; Jun Yeong Lee 2026).

"The Undercover Firm" by Paras and Harshita Adlakha (github.com/ParasAdlakha/sting, Track 3, Q3.2) puts a scripted undercover firm with a report action into a three-firm Bertrand market with four OpenAI models.
